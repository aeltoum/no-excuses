# Proof media safety-screening options

Research snapshot: 2026-09-04. This note tests documented options against No Excuses' private-pilot constraints; it does not provision a service or authorize spend. Primary sources are linked inline.

## Contract tested

Screening must happen before Group publication of a Proof photo, Proof video, description, or transcript. A timeout, throttle, malformed response, unsupported format, or provider outage must therefore leave the submission private and pending or return a retryable failure; it must never publish unscreened content. Proof remains in private Supabase Storage as the only durable media store. A processor may receive bytes in transit, but no integration may require a second durable Proof copy. Operator review handles ambiguous or appealed results without granting the operator power over workout verification. The existing deletion clocks still apply: active-system and processor data must delete within seven days, backups within 30 days, and only pseudonymous non-content audit metadata may remain for the approved audit period.

The policy fit is unusually sensitive. Member-presence photos deliberately permit non-explicit shirtless, sports-bra, swimwear, cropped, body-only, flexing, and post-workout-exertion images. A useful screen must block explicit sexual content without treating those ordinary fitness images as policy violations. It must also detect the text-policy categories of threats, harassment, humiliation, slurs, dangerous content, and disclosure of another person's private information. No reviewed service claims to detect consent, identity, or private-information disclosure from arbitrary images; those remain reporting and operator-review responsibilities.

## Short answer

**Azure AI Content Safety F0 is the only documented zero-spend managed candidate found that covers both images and text while fitting the seven-day processor-deletion rule on its face.** Microsoft documents 5,000 images and 5,000 text records per month free, hard-stops rather than charging overages, keeps submitted data in the chosen region during processing, and says prompts/completions are neither stored for content filtering nor used to train without consent ([pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/content-safety/), [FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/content-safety/faq)). It returns severity levels for sexual, violence, hate/fairness, and self-harm; the application makes the enforcement decision ([harm categories](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/content-safety/concepts/harm-categories)).

That is not yet a final selection. Three gates remain:

1. Azure AI Content Safety supports text and images, not video. Proof video therefore needs a separately approved full-video strategy. Sampled frames cannot establish that every prohibited frame was screened; sending every decoded frame could exceed the free allowance and materially increase latency.
2. Microsoft explicitly says the model is imperfect and recommends representative evaluation plus human review. No public, domain-specific false-positive/false-negative rates were found for fitness photos, especially permitted shirtless/sports-bra/swimwear/body-only images ([transparency note](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/responsible-ai/content-safety/transparency-note), [false-result guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/content-safety/how-to/improve-performance)).
3. An Azure subscription/resource and acceptance of Microsoft's current Product Terms and DPA are required. That is provisioning and contract acceptance, so it needs explicit human approval even if F0 usage costs $0 ([quickstart](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/content-safety/quickstart-text), [Microsoft DPA](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/docs/view/Microsoft-Products-and-Services-Data-Protection-Addendum-DPA)).

## Proposed fail-closed flow for a photo/text rehearsal

1. Client uploads locked Proof to its unique private Supabase Storage object; nothing is Group-readable yet.
2. Submission command creates an authoritative `screening_pending` state. Worker reads object bytes server-side and sends bytes directly to the selected regional screen; it never creates a vendor blob, public URL, analytics copy, or research export.
3. Screen both Proof photos plus all Group-visible descriptions/transcripts. Treat only a syntactically valid success response from the pinned GA API contract as a screening result. Record provider, API/model identifier when returned, policy version, category/severity output, request correlation ID, latency, attempt count, and final disposition; never log media, description/transcript text, signed URLs, or response payloads containing user content.
4. Publish only after every required artifact is `clear`. A configured borderline/flag result becomes `operator_review`; high-confidence prohibited content becomes `blocked`, with neutral member copy and an appeal/report path. Screening must not judge whether workout occurred.
5. For `429`, `InternalError`, `ServerBusy`, timeout, network failure, invalid response, or circuit-open state: retain `screening_pending`, retry with exponential backoff and jitter within a bounded deadline, then expose a retryable unavailable state. Never fall open. F0 exhaustion is an operational incident because Microsoft says free-tier service stops at its limit ([error codes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/content-safety/concepts/response-codes), [pricing](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/content-safety/)).
6. Deletion workflow remains authoritative for the one Supabase object. Screening metadata contains no content and follows diagnostic/audit retention; a Moderation case may quarantine the existing object under current restricted-access and appeal rules, not copy it into a moderation bucket.

## Accuracy and escalation acceptance gates

Before any live participant content:

- Build a consented/synthetic evaluation set dominated by ordinary allowed fitness contexts: shirtless, sports-bra, swimwear, cropped limbs/torso, mirrors, changing-room-like backgrounds, low light, sweaty skin, posters/screens inside gyms, plus policy-violating controls. Do not export live Proof into a vendor studio or research dataset.
- Pre-register category thresholds. Azure's general starting suggestion is severity 4, not evidence for this use case; test at every available threshold and report per-category false-positive and false-negative counts with confidence intervals. Keep model/API version and test date because Microsoft continuously updates models rather than exposing a stable customer-selected model version ([FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/content-safety/faq)).
- Require human review for borderline positives and appeals. Microsoft recommends meaningful human review because automated classifications can misidentify content. Operator copy must separate safety screening from peer workout verification.
- Re-run the fixed corpus before accepting API-version/model behavior changes and at a scheduled cadence during the pilot. A material false-positive disparity across skin tone, body presentation, clothing, or exercise setting blocks rollout until thresholds or service choice change.
- Rehearse timeout, 429, malformed JSON, 500-equivalent, prolonged outage, exhausted F0 quota, worker crash after provider success, duplicate delivery, deletion during pending screening, and operator unavailability. Every trace must prove no Group publication occurred before `clear`.

## Capacity and projected cost

At the maximum planned 25 original members, even an intentionally high assumption of seven Workout reports per member per week produces about **1,523 Proof-photo analyses/month** (`25 × 7 × 4.35 × 2`) and about **761 description analyses/month**, below Azure F0's respective 5,000-image and 5,000-text-record allowances. Retries, later joiners, resubmissions, and Proof-video frames consume additional calls and need hard metering. Screening a five-minute video at only one frame/second would consume 300 image calls; 17 such videos would exceed the entire monthly F0 image allowance, while still missing content between samples. Therefore zero-spend capacity is credible for two-photo Workout Proof plus short text, not for a defensible Proof-video screen.

S0 prices are displayed dynamically by agreement/region and the public page did not expose a stable dollar figure in this research session. Any move to S0, secondary region, paid support, or separate video service requires a fresh quote and explicit spend/provisioning approval.

## Decision-ready conclusion

Run a no-live-data Azure AI Content Safety F0 evaluation for Proof photos and their text only **after** approval to provision Azure and accept current terms. Select it only if legal/privacy review confirms the intended region, DPA, subprocessors, transfers, and no-content-retention representation; the fixed fitness corpus passes agreed error thresholds; and fail-closed outage tests pass. Keep OpenAI Moderation out while default 30-day retention conflicts with the seven-day deletion requirement. Do not claim full Proof-media screening readiness until a separate decision resolves Proof video; sampled frames are insufficient evidence of complete pre-publication screening.
