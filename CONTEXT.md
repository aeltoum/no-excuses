# No Excuses

No Excuses is a group fitness product for friends who are already trying to exercise together and want help staying consistent.

## Language

**Group**:
A small circle of friends who pursue fitness together and hold one another accountable.
_Avoid_: Team, cohort

**Accountability**:
Social or product pressure that makes missed exercise difficult to ignore while preserving each member's voluntary participation and control.
_Avoid_: Unavoidable enforcement, coercion

**Consistency**:
The extent to which a member completes their intended workouts over time, expressed through verified completions, streaks, or adherence to a plan.
_Avoid_: Engagement, app usage

**Weekly target**:
The number of workouts a member intends to complete during a week. Each member sets an individual target while participating in a shared group.
_Avoid_: Group quota, shared workout plan

**Verified completion**:
A workout completion that the product accepts according to its agreed evidence or confirmation rules.
_Avoid_: Check-in, claim

**Missed-target event**:
A neutral, group-visible record created when a member finishes a week below their weekly target. Friends may respond to it in a playful, supportive way.
_Avoid_: Failure, punishment, public shaming

**Consequence card**:
An app-authored, system-selected physical activity assigned in response to a missed-target event. It is drawn at random from cards unlocked by the other group members; members neither create, choose, nor filter eligible cards.
_Avoid_: Punishment, user-selected forfeit

**Card pool**:
The system-assembled set of unique consequence cards eligible for a member's missed-target event. It combines cards unlocked by the other group members, excludes that member's own cards, and gives duplicate unlocks no extra weight.
_Avoid_: Hand-picked deck, weighted duplicates

**Consequence completion**:
A consequence card completed through video evidence approved by two other group members, or by every other member when fewer than two are available.
_Avoid_: Self-reported consequence

**Proof video**:
A group-visible recording submitted as evidence of a consequence completion. It is retained for seven days after approval and then deleted, leaving only the completion record.
_Avoid_: Permanent video archive

**Exception**:
A group-approved waiver for a weekly target affected by illness, injury, travel, or another legitimate circumstance. Members may revise targets themselves before an agreed lock point.
_Avoid_: Excuse, escape
